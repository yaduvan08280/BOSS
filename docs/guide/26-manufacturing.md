# 26. Manufacturing

Install it once: **Masters → Master Library → Packs → Manufacturing → Install**. Nothing you already have is changed. Then fill the tax rates with your accountant (**Money → Money set-up**) — the pack leaves them at 0 %.

## What the pack gives you
Raw material and finished goods by **batch** · a **bill of material** for the finished item · the roles **Production supervisor** and **Quality inspector** · the **Quality check** record · the process **Production order**.

## Buying raw material
Purchase order → goods receipt with the batch (and expiry where it has one).

## Making
1. **Inventory → Recipes & production → New order**: what and how many.
2. **Finish** with what really came out. The raw material leaves the stock by the bill of material; the finished batch comes in at **what it really cost**, and the difference from the plan is kept as the yield variance.
3. An order that needs more raw material than there is cannot be finished.

## Quality
Every batch gets a **Quality check**: checked, rejected, result, defects, a photo. Only an approver **releases** or **holds** it.

## Selling
Bill from **Stock → Sell** (dealer price lists work as in wholesale) or through the Sales CRM's orders.

## The owner looks at
Quality checks by result · stock value · yield variance on production orders · Money owed · Weekly MIS.

## Not in MONO
Machine-wise capacity planning; job cards per operation; costing of labour and power into the batch.
