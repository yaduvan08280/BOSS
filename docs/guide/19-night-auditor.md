# 19. Night auditor

Screen: **Hospitality → Night audit**. Run it once, after the last arrival of the night.

## Before you run it
- Every arrival due today is checked in, or you are sure they are not coming.
- Restaurants and the bar have closed their day.
- Look at **open folios of guests who left** and **unpaid restaurant bills** — they are listed, not blocked.

## What the audit does
1. Bookings due today that did not arrive become **no-shows**, with the plan's charge.
2. Every guest in the house gets tonight's **room charge** with its tax.
3. Every occupied room turns **Dirty**; tomorrow's cleaning is laid out.
4. The **pack** is kept: rooms, sold, occupancy %, ADR, RevPAR, revenue by outlet, money by mode.
5. The hotel's **date moves on**.

It can be run only once for a date. If something was wrong, corrections are made the next day (an allowance, a credit note) — the audit is not undone.

## In the morning
- The **flash report** goes to the owner at 08:00.
- **Form C** due today is on the front desk's list.
