# 25. Wholesale & distribution

Install it once: **Masters → Master Library → Packs → Wholesale & distribution → Install**. Nothing you already have is changed. Then fill the tax rates with your accountant (**Money → Money set-up**) — the pack leaves them at 0 %.

## What the pack gives you
Items with a counter price and a **Dealer price** list · the roles **Order desk** and **Dispatch** · the process **Dealer order to dispatch**.

## Dealers
**Money → Parties**: mark the party *Dealer* and set its **credit days**. A dealer is billed at the dealer price by itself; a walk-in pays the counter price.

## An order
1. Start **Dealer order to dispatch** with the dealer and the items.
2. **Confirm** (check the credit) → **Pick and pack** (tick both lines) → **Dispatch** (the transporter's LR number is required) → **Invoice sent**.
3. Bill the dealer from **Stock → Sell**; the bill is due after the dealer's credit days.

## Collections
**Money → Money owed** shows each dealer by age (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+). Record part payments as receipts; the **statement** shows every bill and payment.

## The owner looks at
Money owed by age · the process report (which step is slow) · the Sales CRM's SCOT sheet for regular dealers · Weekly MIS.

## Not in MONO
Credit-limit blocking at billing (the limit is checked by a person at "Confirm"); scheme accounting with the principal.
