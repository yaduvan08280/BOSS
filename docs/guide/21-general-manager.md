# 21. General manager

What to look at, and when.

## Every morning
- **Flash report** (08:00 message; **Hospitality → Reports → Flash**): sales, guests, spend per guest, occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, food and drink cost %, cash counted against the system, voids, discounts, no-charge bills.
- **Night audit pack**: due-outs, open folios of guests who left, unpaid restaurant bills.
- **Cover board**: who is away and which work has no Buddy.

## Every day
- **Approvals**: voids and discounts above a steward's limit, stock adjustments, count differences, leave.
- **Compliance**: licences running out; temperature readings out of range.
- **Integrations → Connections → Health**: a failed connection, an oversold booking, a payment that was not recorded, a site agent that has gone quiet.

## Every week
- **Reports → Team's week**: each steward's sales and voids, each station's time, each housekeeper's rooms.
- **Reports → Voids & discounts**: who, why, how much.
- **Weekly MIS** and the scorecard for tasks, checklists and process steps.
- **Money owed**: companies and travel agents by age.

## Every month
- **GSTR-1 working file** (Money) for the accountant; **Tally** vouchers posted.
- **Bar register** export checked against the department's form.
- **Stock**: valuation by location, variance, wastage.

## Rules only you (or the owner) change
Discount and void limits by role · who approves · the business-day cut-off · who closes a day · rate plans and restrictions · banquet advance % · dry days.
