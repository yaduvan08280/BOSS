# 16. Bartender

Screens: **POS**, **Kitchen display** (Bar station), **Bar register**, **Inventory**.

## Pouring
- Sell by the **peg** or the **bottle**; the stock is kept in ml, so "11 bottles + 450 ml open" is what the register shows.
- A cocktail takes its recipe: every ingredient leaves the bar's stock when the bill is paid.
- Liquor cannot be sold on a **dry day**: the POS refuses it.

## Receiving and breakage
- Receive bottles in **Inventory → Goods receipts** or a **transfer** from the store; check the count before you accept.
- A broken bottle is **wastage** with the reason "Breakage" the same day.

## The register
- **Bar register** shows each brand's day: opening, received, sold, breakage, closing.
- Count the bar at close. A difference is looked at by the manager before it is posted.
- The **export** is a working file. Check it against your department's form before filing.

## Never
- Never pour without punching it.
- Never top up a bottle from another.
