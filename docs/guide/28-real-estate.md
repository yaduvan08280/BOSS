# 28. Real estate

Install it once: **Masters → Master Library → Packs → Real estate → Install**. Nothing you already have is changed. Then fill the tax rates with your accountant (**Money → Money set-up**) — the pack leaves them at 0 %.

## What the pack gives you
A **Units** master (flat or plot, tower, floor, area, price) · the **Unit booking** record with its **payment schedule** · the roles **Property sales executive** and **Post-sales** · the process **Agreement to registry**.

## Booking a unit
- Add a **Unit booking**: unit, buyer, agreed price, and the schedule (milestone, % of price, due date).
- **One live booking per unit**: a second booking of the same unit is refused. **Cancelling** a booking frees the unit.

## Demands and payments
For each milestone raise an invoice in **Money → Bills** in the buyer's name (with its due date); record payments as receipts. **Money owed** shows every buyer's balance by age.

## Agreement and registry
Run **Agreement to registry** for the unit: draft → buyer signs → stamp duty paid (proof) → registry done (proof). Move the booking to **Agreement signed**, then **Registered**.

## The owner looks at
Bookings by status with their value · Money owed by buyer · the enquiries funnel (Sales CRM → NBD funnel) · Weekly MIS.

## Not in MONO
Demands raised by themselves on a milestone date; interest on late payment; RERA returns.
