# 13. Steward / captain

For the people who take orders at the tables. Screen: **Hospitality → POS**.

## Start of the shift
- Sign in on the tablet or phone. Pick your **outlet** once; it is remembered.
- The **floor** shows every table: Free, Reserved, Seated, Ordered, Billed. Colour is never the only sign — the word is on the table.

## Taking an order
1. Tap a free table → type the **guests** (covers) → **Open**.
2. Tap dishes to add them. A dish with sizes asks **Half / Full**; add-ons are ticked under it.
3. Type a **note** for the kitchen on a line ("less spicy") and the **seat** if the bill will be split by seat.
4. **Send to kitchen**. Each station gets its own ticket. A line you **hold** is not sent until you fire it.
- A dish shown as **86** cannot be ordered: the kitchen is out of it.
- A booked party is seated from **Reservations → Seat**; the order opens on its table by itself.

## Changing an order
- Before it is sent: take the line off, nothing is asked.
- After it is sent: **Void** needs a reason. Above your limit a manager must say yes — the line shows "waiting".
- **Move table**, **join tables** and **move items** are on the order's menu.

## The bill
- **Bill** makes the bill; nothing more can be added to it. Tell the cashier the table.
- The **service charge** is the guest's choice: untick it when the guest says no.
- A **discount** within your limit applies at once; above it, it waits for a manager.

## If the internet drops
Keep working. Lines are kept on the device and sent when the connection is back; a banner says how many wait. Do not sign out until the banner is gone.

## Never
- Never use another person's sign-in.
- Never void to "correct" a bill that is already made — call the manager.
