# 27. Construction

Install it once: **Masters → Master Library → Packs → Construction → Install**. Nothing you already have is changed. Then fill the tax rates with your accountant (**Money → Money set-up**) — the pack leaves them at 0 %.

## What the pack gives you
**Project** (with its bill of quantities), **Running bill** and **Subcontract work order** records · the roles **Site engineer** and **Project manager** · the process **Running bill to the client**.

## A project
Add the project, its client and contract value, and type the **BOQ** lines — MONO works out each line's amount. A project's name is used once.

## Material
Receive cement, steel and sand against purchase orders. **Issue** what is used (Stock → Issue) with a note of the work. Keep a stock location per site (System Master → Branches) to see stock site by site.

## Running bills
1. Add a **Running bill** (RA-1, RA-2 …) with the work done so far and the amount.
2. The project manager **certifies** it; accounts raise the invoice in **Money → Bills** and mark it **Invoiced**.
3. The client's payments are receipts; **Money owed** shows the balance.

## Subcontractors
A **work order** per job; the subcontractor's bill is a purchase bill in Money.

## The owner looks at
Running bills by project · Money owed (clients) and owed (suppliers, subcontractors) · Weekly MIS.

## Not in MONO
Retention money and its release; measurement books line by line against the BOQ; deductions (TDS, labour cess) on bills.
