# 23. Gold & gems

Install it once: **Masters → Master Library → Packs → Gold & gems → Install**. Nothing you already have is changed. Then fill the tax rates with your accountant (**Money → Money set-up**) — the pack leaves them at 0 %.

## What the pack gives you
Every piece by **its own number**, with weight, purity and HUID · the roles **Counter jeweller** and **Vault keeper** · **Old gold purchase** and **Piece certificate** records.

## Every morning
**Inventory → Prices → Rate board**: type today's rate for `GOLD24`. Without today's rate nothing priced by gold can be sold.

## Receiving pieces
**Stock → Receive** each piece with its number, weight in grams, purity and cost. Print or write its barcode. Enter the workshop's bill in **Money → Bills** (purchase bill).

## Selling
- **Scan the piece.** Price = the day's rate × its weight × its purity (22K = 0.916).
- Add **making charges** as a charge on the bill; it carries its own tax code.
- A piece is sold once: it leaves the stock by its number.

## Old gold
Add an **Old gold purchase**: weight, purity, rate — MONO works out the value. Take the scrap into stock. Mark it **Paid / adjusted** when you pay for it or take it off the customer's bill by a receipt.

## Certificates
One **Piece certificate** per piece number and per HUID — a second one is refused.

## The owner looks at
The vault's value (Stock → Value) · old gold by purity · Money owed · Weekly MIS.

## Not in MONO
Old gold cannot yet be netted on the same bill automatically (enter it, then take the difference); hallmarking-portal filing; karigar job-work accounts with wastage.
