# 1. Getting started

## Signing in
- Open the MONO address your company gave you (for GGM: `https://mono.ggminerals.com`).
- Sign in with your **mobile number or email** and password. The first time, MONO asks you to choose your own password.
- Forgot it? Ask an admin: System Master → Users → the lock icon gives you a new one-time password.

## Install it on your phone
- **Android / Chrome / Edge:** a card "Install MONO on this device" appears on top of the page → **Install**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- Then open MONO from the home screen like any app. Turn on notifications in **Profile → Phone notifications**.

## Link Telegram (strongly recommended)
Your tasks, reminders and approvals arrive on Telegram, with buttons (Accept, Done, Move date).
1. Profile → **Link my Telegram** gives an 8-character code.
2. Open the company bot in Telegram, press Start, send the code.
3. You'll see "Your Telegram is linked". Reply to a task message with text to add a note; send a photo as proof.

## Home: "What do you want to do?"
- Type a screen, a task number or a word: `leave`, `stock`, `overdue`, `#70`. **Ctrl+K** opens search anywhere.
- Questions in plain words ("how many orders this month?") go to the AI when your company has connected it.
- **Suggested for you** shows your screens with live counts. Tap the star to keep a screen on top.

## My Workspace
Everything waiting for you in one place: tasks to accept, work due today, things to verify, steps in processes,
Buddy requests and leave to approve. The two counters in the top bar are your pending work and unread notifications.

## Language, look and text size
Profile → Language (English / हिन्दी), Light / Dark / System, Comfortable / Compact, Larger text.

## When something goes wrong
- "Too many requests": wait a minute.
- A screen says "This screen stopped working": press Reload. It has been reported to your admin automatically.
- You are offline: punches are kept on the phone and sent when you are back online; other screens need the internet.
