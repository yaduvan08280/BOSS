# 18. Housekeeping

Screen: **Hospitality → Housekeeping**.

## The board
- Every room with its state: **Dirty**, **Clean**, **Inspected**, **Out of order**, and whether a guest is in it.
- The night audit turns every occupied room Dirty and lays out the day's cleaning.

## Cleaning a room (attendant)
1. Open your room → tick **every line** of the checklist. It cannot be marked done with a line open.
2. **Minibar**: type what was taken — it goes on the guest's bill and leaves the stock.
3. **Done** → the room is Clean and waits for inspection.

## Inspecting (supervisor)
- **Pass** → Inspected: the desk may give the room.
- **Clean again** → back to the attendant with the reason.
- Give rooms out by floor in the morning.

## Problems in a room
- **Out of order**: block the room with the reason and who will fix it. It leaves sale for those days and a ticket is raised. When the ticket is closed the room comes back as Dirty.

## Lost and found
- Enter what was found, where and by whom. Mark it **Returned** (to whom) or **Disposed**.

## Linen and amenities
They leave the housekeeping store as rooms are cleaned. If the store is short, cleaning is never stopped — tell the supervisor.
