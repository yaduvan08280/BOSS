# 6. Processes (FMS)

A process is a fixed chain of steps that every "record" goes through: an **order**, a **complaint**, a **new joiner**.

## Doing your step
- **My FMS steps** → "My steps in FMS": the records waiting for you, with the due time.
- Open the record → fill the step's form, tick its checklist, add proof if asked → **Mark step done**.
- Decisions ask Yes/No; approvals ask Approve/Reject. The next step opens for its doer straight away.
- On leave? Your step goes to its backup, else your Buddy. "Primary on leave · no backup free" alerts the coordinator.

## Starting a record
Live FMS (or "Flows that start with you") → **+ New order** → fill the start form. A public link can also be shared
so customers or vendors fill the start form themselves.

## Watching the work (coordinators)
- **Live FMS:** circles per step with "2 here · 2 late"; tabs Running / Late / Due today / On hold / Closed.
- An hour before a step is due its doer is reminded; when late, the coordinator is told; at twice the planned time,
  the problem solver.
- Record drawer: Timeline (planned vs actual per step), Documents, History. **Reassign the open step**, put on hold,
  resume, close. Records are never edited away: every correction is history.

## Building a process (FMS Builder)
1. **+ New flow** or **Build with AI** (describe how the work goes, in English, Hindi or Hinglish).
2. Add steps: type (task / decision / approval / outside / auto), who, planned time (after previous step, after
   start, before/after a date field), checklist, form fields, documents, stock effect, proof.
3. **Publish** — MONO checks every step has a doer and a time and every decision has both branches. Records keep the
   version they started on; changes make v2.
Fields "from a master" offer a searchable list from Customers, Items, Vendors, People or any master of your own.
