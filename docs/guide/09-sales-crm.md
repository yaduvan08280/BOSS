# 9. Sales CRM

## Plan & setup (owner, once a year)
A 15-page journey: year target and growth → quarters → months → weeks (days add up exactly), business type, whom
you sell to, seats and people, CRR / NBD target split, customer rules, after-order steps, lead sources and funnel,
holidays, weekly off, seasons, round figures. **Send for approval**; the owner approves.

## Every day
- **Today's Calls:** cards with the customer, phone (tap to call), average order and what they usually order.
  Result: **✓ Order** (goes to CRM Orders and starts the after-order steps) · **Later** (pick a date) ·
  **Not now** (next cycle) · **Lost** (why → the customer goes to the win-back list).
- **SCOT sheet:** each regular customer's rhythm: average order, every N days, usual date, the day to call.
- **CRM Orders:** a board with one column per after-order step (confirm, dispatch, documents, payment follow-up,
  payment received). Open a card → mark the step done with documents and proof.
- **Collections:** what each customer owes, by age (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+), promised dates, notes, send the
  statement on WhatsApp.
- **NBD funnel:** new enquiries → **Qualify** (all questions Yes + value + next action) → lead → stages → Won/Lost.
  A lead stuck past its stage's days is flagged.

## Customer Master
Search by name, city or mobile; chips Top 20% · Middle · Bottom 20% · New · Sleeping · Lost · Vampire · Owner's.
Tags are worked out from 12 months of orders and payments. **Fields & tags** adds your own tags and fields.

## Dashboard and MeCA
Month target, achieved, gap and days left; revenue vs target; each sales seat's week graded Green / Yellow / Red.
