# 3. Checklists (recurring work)

A checklist is work that comes back on a schedule: daily machine checks, weekly cleaning, monthly stock count.

## Doing a checklist
1. **My checklists** shows today's runs: name, due time, "0/3 items".
2. Tap the tick → tick each line. Lines may need a photo, a voice note or a value (e.g. a meter reading).
3. **Submit**. If it has a verifier it waits for them; otherwise it is done.
- A reminder comes 30 minutes before the due time. A run not done within its window (default 4 hours) becomes
  **Missed** and the owner is told.

## Making a checklist (HODs / admins)
Checklist master → **New**:
- Name, department/branch, the lines (each may require photo / voice / value).
- **How often:** daily, chosen weekdays, weekly, monthly (a date or "last day"), quarterly, yearly, every N days.
- Due time and window, doer(s) or a role, verifier, "skip holidays and weekly offs", start date.
Runs are created up to 24 hours ahead. Deleting a master keeps its old runs as history.

## Tiles
All · Due today · Overdue (open past the due time) · To verify · Missed (auto-closed, never done).
**Master changes** shows who changed which checklist and when.
