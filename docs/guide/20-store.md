# 20. Store (hospitality)

Screens: **Inventory** → Stock by location, Transfers, Goods receipts, Wastage, Closing counts, Recipes.

## Receiving
- **Goods receipt** against the purchase order: accepted and rejected quantities, batch and expiry where the item needs them.
- A difference in quantity or rate from the order is shown. The latest rate becomes the item's rate.

## Giving to kitchens and bars
- An outlet **asks** (indent); you **approve** and **dispatch**; the outlet **receives**. Stock in between is "in transit".
- Short or damaged on receipt is decided by an approver who did not receive it.
- Between two tax numbers a transfer makes an invoice by itself.

## Rules the system keeps
- **First expired, first out.** An expired batch is never issued — only wasted, returned or counted.
- Stock never goes below zero.
- Bottles and cases: receive by the case, issue by the bottle, sell by the peg — one stock, in ml.

## Counts
- **Closing count** per location. Differences wait for an approver who is not the counter.
- The **variance** compares what was used with what recipes and sales say should have been used.

## Banquets
An event's **indent** lists what its menu needs for the guaranteed guests against your stock. Send it across before the day.
