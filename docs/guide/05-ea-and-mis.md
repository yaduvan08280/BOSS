# 5. EA board and weekly MIS

## EA board (the owner's capture board, run by the EA)
- **Capture** anything in one line: the + button, or send it to the Telegram bot with `/capture`.
- The EA sorts each item: **Information** (for reference), **Someday**, **Waiting** (capped, default 6),
  **Scheduled** (a day and time) or **Delegation** (becomes a task).
- At 08:30 the owner gets the **daily brief**; on Saturday, the weekly plan. Items whose day passed go back to Someday.

## Weekly MIS
Per person, per week, across modules: planned vs done, on-time %, verifications on time, checklist completion and
FMS steps on time. Admins see everyone, HODs their team, everyone else themselves.
It is sent every Monday at 09:00 to the owner and HODs; export PDF or Excel any time.
