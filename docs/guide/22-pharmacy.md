# 22. Pharmacy

Install it once: **Masters → Master Library → Packs → Pharmacy → Install**. Nothing you already have is changed. Then fill the tax rates with your accountant (**Money → Money set-up**) — the pack leaves them at 0 %.

## What the pack gives you
Medicines tracked by **batch and expiry** · the roles **Pharmacist** and **Counter staff** · a **Doctors** master · **Prescription** and **Expiry return** records · the **Schedule H register** · the process "Expiry return to the distributor".

## Buying
1. **Inventory → Purchase** — an order to the distributor.
2. **Goods receipts** — receive it with each **batch number and expiry date**. The distributor's bill is raised when the vendor name is matched to a party.

## Selling at the counter
- Scan the strip or pick the medicine. MONO takes the batch that **expires first**. An **expired batch is never sold** — it can only be returned or written off.
- Cash, UPI or card at once; a hospital or clinic on **credit** (set its credit days on the party).

## Schedule H, H1, X
- Before handing over the medicine, add a **Prescription**: patient, doctor, medicine, quantity, schedule, a photo of the prescription.
- **Mark dispensed** when it is given. The **Schedule H register** (Reports) lists every such sale.

## Near-expiry stock
- **Goods receipts → Return** sends stock back: it leaves your shelf and the distributor's bill is credited.
- Add an **Expiry return** record and run the return through its process so someone chases the credit note.

## The owner looks at
Stock by batch and expiry · the Schedule H register · Money owed (hospitals) and owed to distributors · Weekly MIS.

## Not in MONO
Drug-licence returns to the department; substitution suggestions; insurance claims.
