# 24. Retail shop

Install it once: **Masters → Master Library → Packs → Retail shop → Install**. Nothing you already have is changed. Then fill the tax rates with your accountant (**Money → Money set-up**) — the pack leaves them at 0 %.

## What the pack gives you
Barcoded items with a price · a **Members' price** list · the roles **Shop cashier** and **Store manager** · **Customer return** and **Scheme** records · the **Shop closing** checklist.

## Buying
Purchase order → goods receipt. The shelf count goes up; the distributor's bill is owed.

## Selling
- **Scan** each item; the price comes from the item, or from the **Members' price** when the customer is marked *Member* (Money → Parties).
- A scheme's amount is typed as a **discount** on the bill.
- More than is on the shelf, or an unknown barcode, is refused.

## Returns
Add a **Customer return** with the bill number and the reason. Only a manager **accepts** it. Put the goods back with a stock receipt and give an exchange, a credit note or a refund.

## End of the day
The **Shop closing** checklist: till counted by denomination, card and UPI totals matched. **Money → Day close** compares what was counted with what the bills say.

## The owner looks at
Returns by reason · Money owed · stock below minimum (Stock) · Weekly MIS.

## Not in MONO
Loyalty points; weighing-scale barcodes; buy-one-get-one schemes applied by themselves.
