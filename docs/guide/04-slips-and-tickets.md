# 4. Help slips and help tickets

## Help slip — "here is a problem, and here are my solutions"
1. **Raise a slip:** the problem, and at least the number of solutions your company asks for (normally 3).
2. The **receiver** screens it: forwards it to the approver, or sends it back for more detail.
3. The **approver** picks a solution (or writes their own) → **Approved**. MONO creates a delegation task for it.
4. When that task is done, the raiser **closes** the slip. Each step has a time limit; late steps are reported.

## Help ticket — "something is broken, please fix it"
1. **Raise a ticket:** what is wrong, who should fix it (the problem solver) and by when.
2. The solver replies (first-reply time), fixes it with **proof**, and marks it **Resolved**.
3. You confirm: **Yes, fixed** → Closed, or **No** → back to the solver (the reopen is counted).
- No reply in time, or the fix date passes → **escalated**: level 1 to the coordinator, level 2 to the HOD and owner.
- "Who closes the ticket" is set by the admin: the raiser confirms, or it closes by itself after 24 hours.
