# 15. Kitchen

Screen: **Hospitality → Kitchen display**. Pick your **station** once.

## Tickets
- Each ticket shows the table, the lines, notes and how long it has waited.
- **White** = new, **amber** from 10 minutes, **red** from 20. The minutes are written on the ticket too.
- Tap a line when it is ready; **Ready** marks the whole ticket; **Bump** takes it off the screen when it has left the pass.
- **Recall** brings back a ticket bumped by mistake.
- A voided line is struck through. "Cooked" voids are written off the stock as waste.

## Running out (86)
- Tell the manager, or switch the dish **off** in **Menu**. It disappears from the POS at once.
- A dish also switches itself off when its ingredients run out at your location.

## Stock
- **Wastage** (spoilt, burnt, expired) is entered the same day in **Inventory → Wastage**, with the reason. Small amounts apply at once; large ones wait for an approver.
- **Closing count** at the end of the day or week: count what is on the shelf. A blind count hides the system's figure.
- Indent what you need from the store in **Transfers → Ask**.

## Hygiene log
- **Compliance → Temperatures**: log each fridge and freezer. A reading outside its limits needs what you did about it.

## If the printer stops
The screen still shows every ticket. Tell the manager; tickets are printed again when the printer is back.
