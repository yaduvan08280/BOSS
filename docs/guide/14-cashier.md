# 14. Cashier

Screens: **Hospitality → POS** (bills, payments) and **Bills & day close**.

## Opening
- **Open your shift** and type the **float** you were given.

## Taking payment
1. Open the billed table → **Pay bill**.
2. Pick the **way of paying**: cash, UPI, card, room, company. Several ways can be used on one bill.
3. UPI and card ask for a **reference** (the UTR or the slip number).
4. **Room charge**: type the room number. It goes on the guest's folio; nobody checked in → it is refused.
5. **Company credit** only on a bill in the company's name.
- The printed bill has a **QR code** for UPI. "Send a payment link" makes a link for the guest's phone (when a gateway is connected).
- A **tip** is typed with the payment and goes to the steward — it is not part of the bill.
- Never take more than the bill: the screen refuses it.

## Splitting
- **By item** or **by seat** makes separate bills. **Equally** or **by amounts** keeps one bill paid in parts.

## Mistakes
- A bill made wrongly is **cancelled by a manager** with a reason; its payments are undone and the table opens again.
- A paid order is never cancelled at the POS: accounts raise a credit note.

## Delivery partners (Zomato / Swiggy)
- On a takeaway or delivery order, **mark the order** with the partner and their order number before the bill.
- Close the bill on **Aggregator** — no money is taken; the partner pays later.

## Closing
1. **Close your shift**: count your cash.
2. The manager closes the **day**: every table billed and paid, cash counted **by denomination**. With a blind count you do not see the expected cash until it is closed.
3. A closed day takes nothing more. Only the owner reopens it, with a reason.
