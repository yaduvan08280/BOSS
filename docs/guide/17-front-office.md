# 17. Front office

Screens: **Front desk**, **Room calendar**, **Room rates**.

## The hotel's date
The hotel has its own **business date**. It moves only when the night audit is run — after midnight the desk still works on yesterday's date until then.

## A booking
1. **New booking**: rate plan, room type, dates, guests. The price of every night is shown before you save.
2. No room left, a stop-sell or a minimum stay → the screen says which.
3. A room can be given now or at check-in. The **room calendar** shows who is where.
- Bookings from travel sites arrive by themselves and are marked with the site. One marked **oversold** has no room: move, upgrade or relocate the guest.
- Your own **booking page** takes bookings from guests directly.

## Check-in
- Pick a clean room, take the **ID** (photo of it) and the **signed registration card**.
- A guest who is not Indian needs **Form C** within 24 hours: fill passport, visa and travel details. The desk is reminded.
- Early arrival and late departure charges follow the property's rules.

## During the stay
- **Charges**: laundry, extras, minibar go on the folio. Restaurant bills "to the room" arrive by themselves.
- **Advance**: take it at the desk, or **Send a payment link**.
- **Room move** needs a reason; an upgrade may be free or charged from that night.
- A request from the room's QR page becomes a ticket for the right person.

## Check-out
1. Open the **folio**: charges, tax, advances, what is due.
2. Take the balance; a company's folio may be closed **on credit**.
3. Closing the folio makes the **GST invoice**. Every folio must be closed before check-out.

## Cancellations and no-shows
The plan's rule sets the charge. It is put on the folio and settled like any charge.
