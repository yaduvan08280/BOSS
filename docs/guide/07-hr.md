# 7. HR: attendance, leave, Buddy and pay

## Punching
- Attendance → **Punch in** / **Punch out**. MONO reads your location (and a selfie if your company asks).
- Away from the office, or location off: depending on the rules, the punch is blocked or allowed and flagged.
- No internet? The punch is kept on the phone and sent when you are online again.
- Forgot a punch? **Correction request** (a few per month) → your HOD approves.

## How a day is counted
Late after the grace minutes; **half day** when very late or short of the full-day hours; **absent** under half of
them. A missed punch-out is a half day until it is corrected. Days are settled two hours after the shift ends.

## Leave
1. Leave → **Apply**: type, from–to, half day, reason.
2. MONO lists your open work in those days and asks for a **Buddy** for it. The Buddy answers Yes / Can't (also on
   Telegram).
3. Your HOD approves (or MONO does for short leave, if the rules say so). Balances update at once.
On the first day of leave your Buddy becomes the doer ("Covered by …"); when you are back, the work returns by the rule.

## Cover board (HODs, HR)
Today's absentees, their open work and Buddy status (asked / accepted / none), with one-click cover. The morning
report at 08:30 lists who is away and which work has no Buddy.

## Pay from attendance (HR only)
Payroll shows **gross pay** per person for the month from attendance and the pay rules (month / day / hour rates,
overtime, late cuts). Close the month when every day is settled. PF, ESI, TDS and payslips come later.
