# 29. Logistics & transport

Install it once: **Masters → Master Library → Packs → Logistics & transport → Install**. Nothing you already have is changed. Then fill the tax rates with your accountant (**Money → Money set-up**) — the pack leaves them at 0 %.

## What the pack gives you
**Vehicles** and **Drivers** masters · **Consignment note** and **Trip** records · the roles **Traffic desk** and **Driver** · the process **Trip expense claim**.

## A consignment
1. Add a **Consignment note**: consignor, consignee, from, to, weight, freight, the vehicle (from the master).
2. **Loaded and left** → **Delivered (POD)**.
3. Raise the **freight bill** in Money → Bills in the consignor's name.

## A trip
Add a **Trip**: vehicle, driver, route, start and end km (MONO works out the kilometres), the advance, and the **expenses** (toll, allowance …) with their bills. **Start**, then **Close**.

## Expenses
The driver's claim runs through **Trip expense claim**: bills handed in (proof) → supervisor checks → accounts settle.

## Fuel
Enter the pump's bills as purchase bills in Money; they show under **Money owed → Payable**.

## The owner looks at
Freight by vehicle · Money owed by consignor · trips and their kilometres · Weekly MIS.

## Not in MONO
E-way bill filing; GPS; a vehicle's bookings by time (two notes can name one vehicle for the same day — the traffic desk decides); tyre and service schedules (use Checklists).
