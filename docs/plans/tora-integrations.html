Connecting MONO to Booking.com, Agoda, District and Zomato

How room bookings, rates, table reservations, food orders and payments from outside platforms can land in MONO and be managed from one place, what MONO already has, what to build, and in which order.

The short answer

You will not connect MONO to each platform one by one. There are three doors, and each needs a partner:

  1. Hotel rooms (Booking.com, Agoda, MakeMyTrip/Goibibo, Expedia, Airbnb): connect MONO to one channel manager such as AxisRooms, STAAH, eZee Centrix, Aiosell or SiteMinder. It already holds certified links to every OTA. MONO sends rooms left, rates and restrictions to it, and receives bookings, changes and cancellations back. Getting Booking.com certification directly is slow and often closed to new partners.
  2. Food delivery (Zomato, Swiggy): Zomato has an official POS developer platform with menu, order and outlet APIs, so MONO can register as a POS vendor. Swiggy gives order APIs only to approved POS partners. Until you are approved, a middleware such as UrbanPiper can relay both.
  3. Table bookings (District, EazyDiner, Swiggy Dineout): none of them publishes a reservation API. Ask each one's partner team for access. Until then, MONO can create a reservation automatically from each booking confirmation email, tagged with its source, so the floor still sees every booking.

Payments (deposits, advances, OTA payouts and aggregator settlements) come in through a payment gateway such as Razorpay or Cashfree, plus settlement files that MONO reconciles against bills.

Starting point

What MONO already has (Phase 19)

Channel manager contract

MONO's own plain JSON contract: POST {base}/ari sends rooms left, price, stop-sell, closed to arrival and minimum stay. GET {base}/bookings?since= pulls bookings, and the webhook /api/hooks/channel/<token> receives them. Bookings are made once per channelRef, never refused, and flagged overbooked when the house is full.

Payments

A gateway connection (keys encrypted with APP_SECRET), payment links for deposits and advances, a UPI QR on printed bills, and receipts set against bills.

Aggregator orders

POS orders can be tagged aggregator + aggregatorRef. A Zomato/Swiggy payout file can be checked and recorded against those orders.

Booking page & e-invoice

A public room-booking page per property (/rooms/<token>), a table-booking page per outlet, a GST e-invoice adapter, and the site agent for printers and Tally.

Gaps named in CLAUDE.md §18: nothing has been tried against a live account. The channel manager and e-invoice adapters speak MONO's own contract, so each real provider needs a small mapping. Not built yet: direct order push from Zomato/Swiggy, refunds through the gateway, and e-way bills.

1 · Rooms: map the channel manager onto MONO's contract

  • Keep MONO's internal contract and add one adapter per provider (connect/providers/axisrooms.ts …). It turns MONO's ARI rows into the provider's request and the provider's booking into MONO's booking.
  • Mapping tables: room type code ↔ provider room ID, rate plan code ↔ provider rate ID, per channel where the provider needs it. These live on the CHANNEL connection's meta.
  • Push ARI on every change that alters rooms left or a price (bookings, blocks, out of order, rate edits), debounced to one push per room type per day range, with a full refresh each night after the night audit.
  • Booking in: create the Stay with source: OTA, channel, channelRef, the OTA's price kept in the note, and commission on an AGENT rate plan. Modify moves the nights; cancel goes through the stay machine.
  • Payments: many OTA bookings are prepaid with a virtual card (VCC). MONO must never store card numbers. Read the card only inside the channel manager's PCI vault, and charge it on the gateway or card machine. Record the receipt in MONO with a reference.

2 · Food delivery: Zomato as a POS integration

  • New connection kind AGGREGATOR per outlet: keys, outlet ID on Zomato, an auto-accept switch and the prep time.
  • Order webhook → POS order (type: DELIVERY, aggregator: ZOMATO, aggregatorRef). Items are matched by a mapping (menu item ↔ Zomato item/variant/add-on ID), KOTs fire to the stations, and accept/reject goes back to Zomato. Status updates follow the kitchen display: ready → handed over.
  • Menu push from MONO's menu, with prices per outlet and availability windows. 86 sync: when an item is switched off by a person or by stock, MONO marks it out of stock on Zomato.
  • Outlet status: opening hours from the outlet; a "pause orders" button on the POS for rush hours.
  • Settlement: the weekly payout file is matched against the orders' aggregatorRef, and the commission and TCS/TDS lines are booked (the payout import already exists).

3 · Table bookings from District and EazyDiner

  • Ask both partner teams for an API or a booking feed. If they give one, add it as a RESERVATION_PARTNER connection.
  • Until then, use a mail-in parser. Forward booking confirmation emails to a dedicated inbox such as bookings@…. MONO reads the guest name, phone if present, party size, date, time and booking ID, then creates a reservation with source: ONLINE plus a partner tag. Duplicates are refused by the booking ID, and a cancellation email cancels the booking.
  • EazyDiner PayEazy and District payments are already a payment mode in TORA's set-up ("EazyDiner / District pay"), so their settlements reconcile like card settlements.
Order of work

Roadmap

A
Pick the channel manager (1 week). Ask AxisRooms, STAAH, eZee Centrix, Aiosell and SiteMinder for their PMS API docs, a test account, their certification steps for a new PMS, and their coverage of Booking.com, Agoda and MMT. Choose the one with a documented, two-way PMS API and an Indian support team.
B
Rooms adapter + certification (3–5 weeks). Write the provider mapping on MONO's contract, the mapping screen, debounced ARI push, booking pull plus webhook, and modify/cancel. Pass the channel manager's PMS certification, then switch on Tora Stays with Booking.com and Agoda.
C
Zomato POS integration (4–6 weeks). Register on the developer platform. In the sandbox, build menu push, the order webhook to POS, accept/reject/status, 86 sync, outlet status and settlement matching. Then go live at the Murlipura kitchen.
D
Swiggy (depends on approval). Apply to the POS partner programme. If it is slow, connect through middleware with the same order pipeline as Zomato.
E
District / EazyDiner (1–2 weeks for the mail-in parser). Start partner conversations in parallel.
F
Payments & reports (2 weeks). Gateway refunds, VCC handling through the channel manager vault, and a channel-mix report: covers, rooms and revenue by source, with commission and net revenue.
Before going live

Tax, data and compliance

  • GST on aggregator food orders: since 01/01/2022, Zomato and Swiggy collect and pay the 5% GST on restaurant services sold through them (section 9(5)). So MONO must bill aggregator orders without charging GST again, and record them as supplies through an e-commerce operator. The accountant should confirm before go-live.
  • Rooms: GST by tariff slab (5% up to ₹7,500 a night, 18% above, after the September 2025 rate change). The OTA's commission invoice carries its own GST (input credit rules apply). TCS by the e-commerce operator shows on payout statements.
  • Card data: PCI DSS. MONO never stores card numbers or CVV. Virtual cards are read in the channel manager's vault.
  • Guest data: the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Keep marketing consent per guest (MONO already logs consent) and only use OTA guest data for the stay.
  • Foreign guests: Form C within 24 hours. MONO's rule already chases it, and OTA bookings of foreign nationals should flag it at check-in.
  • Liquor: never list liquor on delivery apps, which aren't licensed to sell it. Keep it off the Zomato menu push by category.
Checklist

What to ask each partner

Channel manager

  • Do you have a documented PMS API with ARI push and booking push/pull?
  • How do you certify a new PMS, and how long does it take?
  • Which OTAs do you cover in India (Booking.com, Agoda, MMT/Goibibo, Expedia, Airbnb)?
  • How do you handle virtual cards and PCI?
  • What does it cost for 18 rooms, and is the API included?

Zomato / Swiggy

  • Can MONO join as a POS vendor (sandbox, go-live checks)?
  • Webhook signing, retries and order timeouts?
  • How are variants, add-ons and combos mapped?
  • How does stock-out sync work, and what are the rate limits?
  • What format are payout and settlement reports in, and can they be fetched by API?

District / EazyDiner

  • Is there a reservation API or a booking webhook for POS partners?
  • Can we receive a daily booking feed (CSV or email)?
  • How are cover charges and PayEazy payments settled, and how often?
